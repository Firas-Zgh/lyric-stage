    How it works

    1. Plug the iPhone into the monitor's HDMI port, power the monitor from USB-C, and stand it up.
    2. Pick a song and tap Show.
    3. Turn the phone sideways. The lyrics fill the monitor, upright. Tap the phone screen for controls.

    Words upside down on the monitor? Switch Monitor to the other vertical setting below. Portrait Orientation Lock has to be off. The page keeps the phone awake while lyrics are up; if it still locks, set Auto-Lock to Never while you play.

    Paste lyrics to see how they will play.

    Plain lyrics scroll at your speed. Lyrics with times like [00:12.40] (LRC files) light up line by line. Lines like [Chorus] become section labels. A chord line right above a lyric line keeps each chord over its word, and lines in (parentheses) show as backing vocals.
